# Privacy Policy – SuraSole Diabetic

**Effective date:** November 18, 2026

[ภาษาไทย](./th)

SuraSole Diabetic ("the App") is provided by Suratec Co., Ltd. ("we", "us", "our"). The App works with SuraSole smart insoles to help people with diabetes and their healthcare providers monitor foot pressure, balance, gait and foot health. This policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you provide
- **Account information:** username, password, email address, gender, and account type (patient or healthcare provider).
- **Profile information:** first and last name, age, phone number, height, weight and a profile photo (optional).
- **Health and medical information** you choose to enter, including:
  - medical history such as diabetes, hypertension and dyslipidemia, and how long you have had them, other diseases and past injuries (ankle, knee, hip);
  - use of walking aids (cane, crutch, walker, wheelchair);
  - daily records such as blood sugar level, meals and hours of sleep;
  - results of foot sensation (monofilament) tests.
- **Foot photos** you take or select for foot assessment.
- **Chatbot messages:** text messages and voice recordings you send to the in-app health assistant.
- **Orders:** the insoles or shoes you order through the App and your order history.

### Information collected from the SuraSole insoles
When you connect your insoles over Bluetooth, the App reads **foot pressure data** and uses it to produce pressure maps, balance, gait and walking-training results. These measurements and results are uploaded to our servers so that you, and the healthcare providers you work with, can review them.

### Information collected automatically
- **Device information:** device type and a push-notification token, used to deliver notifications to your device.

### Device permissions
| Permission | Why the App asks for it |
|---|---|
| Bluetooth | Connect to your SuraSole insoles and read pressure data, including while the App is in the background during a recording. |
| Location (while using the App) | Required by the operating system to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices. **We do not store or upload your location.** |
| Camera | Take your profile photo and photos of your feet. |
| Photo library | Choose an existing photo for your profile or foot assessment. |
| Microphone | Record voice messages you send to the chatbot. |
| Notifications | Send you reminders and updates. |

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings. Some features will not work without them.

## 2. How we use your information
We use your information to:
- create and manage your account and sign you in;
- connect to your insoles and show your foot-pressure, balance, gait and training results;
- store your health records, daily data and foot photos so you and your healthcare provider can track your foot health over time;
- answer your questions through the chatbot;
- process your orders for insoles or shoes;
- send you push notifications;
- maintain, secure and improve the App.

We **do not** sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

## 3. How we share your information
We share your information only as follows:
- **Healthcare providers:** if you use the App as a patient, healthcare provider accounts that are linked to you in the App can view your profile, health records and measurement results.
- **Service providers:** companies that help us run the App, under contracts that limit what they can do with the data. These include **Google Firebase** (push notifications) and the cloud services that host our servers and chatbot.
- **Legal reasons:** when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, us or others.

## 4. Where your data is stored and how it is protected
Your data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to servers operated by or for Suratec Co., Ltd. Access to the data is limited to authorized staff and the healthcare providers linked to your account. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

## 5. How long we keep your data
We keep your information for as long as your account is active, or as long as we need it to provide the service. After you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information, unless we are required by law to keep it longer.

## 6. Your rights and choices
Under the Thai Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and other applicable laws, you may:
- access and get a copy of your personal data;
- correct inaccurate data (you can edit most of it in the App's Profile screen);
- ask us to delete your account and personal data;
- withdraw your consent or object to certain processing.

To make a request, including deleting your account, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the period required by law.

## 7. Medical disclaimer
The App supports monitoring and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider about your health.

## 8. Children
The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will also notify you in the App.
